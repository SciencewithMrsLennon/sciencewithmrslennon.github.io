Mission 3 of 5

✈️ Steer the Jet Stream

The jet stream is a fast river of wind about 9–12 km up, flowing west to east. It acts like a fence: cold polar air stays north of it, and warmer air stays south. Use the sliders to bend the jet stream and complete each challenge.
