Newton's Three Laws — Quick Reference

1st Law · Inertia

An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion keeps moving at the same speed and direction, unless an unbalanced force acts on it.

2nd Law · F = m × a

The acceleration of an object depends on the net force and the mass. More force → more acceleration. More mass → less acceleration.  a = F ÷ m

3rd Law · Action–Reaction

When one object pushes on another, the second object pushes back with an equal force in the opposite direction.
