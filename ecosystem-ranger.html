Ranger Field Guide

Food web: all the connected food chains in an ecosystem. Arrows point the way energy flows, from the food to the eater.

Disruption: a population change, natural disaster or human action that changes how energy moves through a food web.

Succession: the slow, predictable change in which species live in an area after a disruption. Primary succession starts on bare rock with no soil. Secondary succession starts where soil is left behind.
