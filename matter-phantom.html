STAAR BOSS BATTLE · TEKS 8.6E

THE MATTER
PHANTOM

A ghost is haunting the chemistry lab, swearing that matter can just vanish during chemical reactions. Only a class that truly understands conservation of mass and chemical equations can send it back where it came from.

How to play (teacher projects, whole class plays):
  1. Project this screen. Students answer each question on mini whiteboards or scrap paper.
  2. When time is up, students hold up their answers. Click the answer most of the class chose (or press A–D).
  3. Correct = the class hits the Phantom. 3 in a row = COMBO damage!
  4. Wrong = the Phantom smashes a class shield. Lose all 5 and the Phantom recharges.
  5. Read the explanation together, then click Next (or press Space). 24 questions · about 45 minutes.
