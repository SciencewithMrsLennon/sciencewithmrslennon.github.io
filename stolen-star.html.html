Case briefing

The evidence has gone missing.

This is a fictional, nonviolent mystery. Determine who moved the papers, which room they took them to, and what they used to carry them. The science questions unlock elimination clues; they are puzzle evidence, not forensic measurements.

How to investigate

  1. Read the briefing and candidate board (4 minutes).
  2. Work through all 6 stations (about 6 minutes each). Answer both questions, record the linked clues, and explain your science evidence.
  3. Use your provisional case board to identify one remaining candidate in each category. Wrong science choices can produce conflicting clues; revisit your work before submitting.
  4. Complete your accusation and submit once for grading (5–10 minutes). Science answers and the three mystery selections are scored; written explanations are teacher-reviewed.

No account or internet connection is needed after this file is downloaded. Work stays on this browser/device when storage is permitted. No results are sent to a teacher automatically. This is a review activity, not a secure testing platform.

Turn in your results

  1. Make sure your name/ID, case title, and score are visible. Close the answer review before taking a screenshot.
  2. Chromebook: Ctrl + Shift + Show windows; choose Screenshot and select the results. Windows: Windows + Shift + S; select the results and save the capture. Mac: Command + Shift + 4; drag around the results.
  3. Screenshot option: open the Google Classroom assignment. Under Your work, choose Add or create → File, upload the saved screenshot, then Turn in.
  4. Private-comment option: if your teacher allows it, use the assignment's Private comments box. Enter the format below and send/post it. Also select Turn in or Mark as done when directed; a comment alone does not automatically turn in work.

You may also use Print / Save as PDF or Download results. Avoid posting your results as a public class comment. Follow your teacher's choice of submission method.
