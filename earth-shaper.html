Earth Shaper Lab

Welcome, junior geologist! Earth's surface is broken into giant plates that are always moving. In this lab, you move them. Push plates together, pull them apart and slide them past each other to see how plate tectonics builds mountains, opens oceans, sets off earthquakes and fuels volcanoes.

Field Guide: three kinds of plate boundaries

Convergent: plates push together. Mountains, trenches, volcanoes and strong earthquakes.

Divergent: plates pull apart. Magma rises, new crust forms, oceans open.

Transform: plates slide past each other. Faults and earthquakes, but almost no volcanoes.
