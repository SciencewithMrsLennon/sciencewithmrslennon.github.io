STAAR BOSS BATTLE · TEKS 8.7A & 8.7B

THE INERTIA
TITAN

A giant robot that refuses to change its motion has taken over the science lab. Only a class that truly understands force and Newton's laws can bring it down.

How to play (teacher projects, whole class plays):
  1. Project this screen. Students answer each question on mini whiteboards or scrap paper.
  2. When time is up, students hold up their answers. Click the answer most of the class chose (or press A–D).
  3. Correct = the class hits the Titan. 3 in a row = COMBO damage!
  4. Wrong = the Titan smashes a class shield. Lose all 5 and the Titan recharges.
  5. Read the explanation together, then click Next (or press Space). 24 questions · about 45 minutes.
