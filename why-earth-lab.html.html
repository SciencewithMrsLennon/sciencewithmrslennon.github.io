      📖 Field Guide

      Habitable (Goldilocks) Zone
      The range of distances from a star where a planet is not too hot and not too cold for liquid water.
      Astronomical Unit (AU)
      Earth's average distance from the Sun, about 150 million km.
      Liquid water
      Every living thing we know needs it. It dissolves nutrients, carries materials, holds heat, and is where cell reactions happen.
      Atmosphere
      The layer of gases surrounding a planet. Its thickness (air pressure) and composition matter.
      Composition
      What something is made of. Earth's air is about 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, and 1% other gases like argon and carbon dioxide.
      Greenhouse effect
      Gases like carbon dioxide (CO₂) trap heat near a planet's surface. A little keeps Earth warm; a lot makes a planet like Venus scorching.
      Ozone layer
      A layer of ozone gas (made from oxygen) high in Earth's atmosphere that absorbs harmful ultraviolet (UV) rays.
      Air pressure
      The push of the atmosphere on the surface. If it is too low, liquid water quickly turns to vapor and escapes.
