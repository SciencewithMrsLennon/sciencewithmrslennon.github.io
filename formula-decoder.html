Station 3 · Multiply or Add?

TEKS 7.6B
Decoder Briefing: Some numbers make copies. A big number in front copies the whole formula. A number after parentheses copies what is inside. Copies mean ✖ MULTIPLY. Putting different elements together for a total means ➕ ADD.

🧮 Count Coach

📏 Counting Rules

Small number after a symbolCounts only the ONE atom right before it. No number = 1.
✖ Big number in FRONTCopies of the whole formula. MULTIPLY every atom.
✖ Number after ( )Copies of what is inside. MULTIPLY only the atoms inside.
➕ Same element in 2 placesADD those counts together.
➕ "Total atoms"ADD the counts of every element.
🔦 "How many CARBON atoms?"Count ONLY carbon. Ignore every other element.
